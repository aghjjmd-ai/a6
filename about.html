About Toewear

We think socks deserve a little more thought.

Socks are worn every day, for hours at a time, yet most of us buy them without a second look. Toewear exists to change that.

We explain fibres, sizes, heights and care in plain, friendly language. Our three tools give you a quick answer; our guides explain the reasons behind it.
